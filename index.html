Unfurl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Unfurl is a browser extension for capturing, annotating, and exporting full-page screenshots. This page describes exactly what data Unfurl accesses, stores, and sends — in full, not as marketing copy.

What Unfurl accesses

Unfurl only reads the content of a web page when you explicitly trigger a capture (clicking the toolbar button, using the keyboard shortcut, or the right-click menu). It does not run on pages you haven't asked it to capture, and it does not read page content in the background.

What Unfurl stores

Everything Unfurl stores stays on your device, in your browser's local storage:

  • Captured screenshots — held in the browser's IndexedDB until you export them (as PNG/PDF) or close the capture, then discarded.
  • Settings — your preferences (capture delay, default export format, etc.) via chrome.storage.local.
  • License key (if you activate Pro features) — stored locally and verified entirely on your device using public-key cryptography. No license key, account, or identifying information is ever sent to any server.

What Unfurl sends over the network

Nothing. Unfurl makes no network requests of any kind — no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no "phone home" of any form. Everything — capture, annotation, export, and license verification — happens locally in your browser.

What Unfurl shares with third parties

Nothing. There are no third parties involved in how Unfurl works.

Permissions Unfurl requests, and why

  • activeTab — to read the page you're actively capturing, only when you trigger a capture.
  • scripting — to inject the capture logic into that tab, on demand.
  • downloads — to save your exported PNG/PDF files.
  • storage — for local settings and license state, described above.
  • contextMenus — to add a right-click "Capture" entry.
  • clipboardWrite — for the "Copy to clipboard" export option.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, and any change will be reflected in the extension's store listing before it takes effect.
